PEN-PAL PET 2.0.0  -  Windows

A small pet carries short notes and doodles between computers on the same
Wi-Fi. No account, no server, nothing to sign up for.


HOW TO INSTALL

  1. Double-click  Pen-pal-Pet-Setup-2.0.0.exe

  2. Windows shows a blue box: "Windows protected your PC".
     Click  More info  ->  Run anyway
     This appears because the app has no paid code-signing certificate.
     It means the publisher is unverified, not that anything is wrong.

  3. Read the terms, click  I Agree

  4. Choose who it is for, click  Next

  5. Choose where to put it (or keep the suggestion), click  Install

  6. Click  Finish. The app opens.

No administrator password is needed.


FIRST RUN

  Type your name and pick a pet.

  Windows asks whether to allow Pen-pal Pet through the firewall.
  Say YES, and tick PRIVATE NETWORKS ONLY.
  Without this your friend's computer cannot hand notes to yours.
  Never tick Public networks - the app has no business listening on a
  cafe or airport network.


USING IT

  Install it on a second computer on the same Wi-Fi and the two find each
  other by themselves. Write a note, doodle on it, and your pet walks it
  over. It comes back with the reply.

  To try it alone, choose "Me (test run)" in the To list and watch the
  whole trip in one window.


IS IT SAFE?

  Notes are encrypted between the two computers and every message is
  signed, so nobody else on the Wi-Fi can read or alter them. Your keys
  and your notes are sealed on disk using Windows' own encryption.

  Display names are not unique - anyone can call themselves anything.
  The four words shown beside each friend are what tell a real friend
  from an imitator. Read them aloud to each other once.

  The app makes no internet connections at all.


TO REMOVE IT

  Settings  ->  Apps  ->  Installed apps  ->  Pen-pal Pet  ->  Uninstall

  Your notes are kept in case you reinstall. To clear those too, delete
  the folder:  %APPDATA%\Pen-pal Pet
